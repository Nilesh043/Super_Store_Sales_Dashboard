# Super Store Sales Dashboard (Excel)
An interactive sales dashboard built in Excel using the Super Store dataset. Before building it, I defined 10 business questions and designed every KPI and chart to answer them.

## Project Overview
The dashboard gives a quick view of sales performance across regions, years, shipping modes, categories and customer segments. Two slicers (Region and Year) let anyone filter the data, and every KPI and chart updates instantly.

## Business Questions Answered
1. Total sales of all transactions
2. Average sales per transaction
3. Total number of transactions
4. Total quantity sold
5. Sales by ship mode
6. Transaction count by region
7. Month-wise quantity sold
8. Top 5 cities by sales
9. Category-wise transaction count
10.Customer-type-wise quantity ordered

## Key Insights
 - Shipping: Standard Class accounts for 58% of sales, while Same Day is only 6%. Customers favour cost over speed.
 - Regions: The West leads with 3,203 transactions. The South has the fewest (1,620), roughly half of the West, making it the biggest growth opportunity.
 - Seasonality: Quantity ordered is lowest in January and February and peaks in September and November. October shows a dip worth investigating.
 - Cities: New York City is the top city by sales (6,54,439), well ahead of Los Angeles (4,20,553).
 - Categories: Office Supplies drives the most transactions (6,026), followed by Furniture (2,121) and Technology (1,847).
 - Customer segments: Consumers order the most units (13,030), followed by Corporate (7,598) and Home Office (4,495).

## Dashboard Features
 - 4 KPI cards (Total Sales, Average Sales, Transactions, Total Quantity)
 - 6 charts: pie, donut, line and column/bar charts
 - Interactive slicers for Region and Year (Order Date), covering 2014 to 2017
 - Clean, single-page layout designed for non-technical users

## Tools Used
   - Microsoft Excel
   - Pivot Tables
   - Pivot Charts
   - Slicers
   - Power Query

## Dataset
Super Store sales dataset (orders from 2014 to 2017) with fields such as Order Date, Ship Mode, Region, City, Category, Customer Segment, Sales and Quantity.

## How to Use
 1. Download and open the Excel file.
 2. Use the Region and Years (Order Date) slicers at the top to filter the data.
 3. Watch the KPIs and charts update together.
 4. Click the filter-clear icon beside each slicer to reset.
